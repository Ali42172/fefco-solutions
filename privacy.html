FEFCO Solutions – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

FEFCO Solutions ("FEFCO", "we", "us") provides solar water heater, solar PV, plumbing, maintenance and AMC services in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us (by phone call, WhatsApp, our website, Facebook or Instagram), how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, mobile number, email address, area or villa address.
  • Details about your request: the service you need, your system details, preferred visit or call time, and notes you share with us.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram, and the delivery and read status of messages we send you.
  • Phone calls with our team or our automated phone assistant, which may be recorded and summarised so we can book and prepare your visit.
  • Advertising information passed to us when you respond to our Facebook or Instagram ads (for example the ad you clicked or the form you filled in).

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, call you back and book a site visit or phone consultation.
  • To send you booking confirmations, reminders and updates by WhatsApp, email or phone call.
  • To let our technicians and team prepare for and attend your appointment.
  • To improve our service and keep records required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Google Calendar data

Our booking system connects to FEFCO's own Google Calendar only to check which appointment times are free and to add, move or cancel the appointments you book with us. This data is used only for scheduling FEFCO appointments, is not shared with third parties except as needed to provide this feature, and is not used for advertising. FEFCO's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Service providers we use

We share information only with providers that help us run our service: Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram), Google (Calendar and email), our phone-assistant and calling provider, and our secure database and automation hosting. They process the data on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete or anonymise them.

6. Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from us, or tell us on a call, and we will stop contacting you.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time.
